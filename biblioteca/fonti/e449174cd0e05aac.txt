Wide semi-realistic literary fantasy illustration, archaeological reconstruction of three separate ancient operating stations across a broad inhabited upland basin, each visibly a distinct modest complex linked by ordinary paths and channels. At the nearest station, stone benches, metal-and-glass support apparatus and covered sheltered walkways for patients; the distant two stations sit apart on opposing terraces. No giant central machine, no single omnipotent portal, no levitating city, no apocalypse, no crowds dying, no identifiable story characters. Beautiful late-antique fantasy engineering and serene tense dusk, clear landscape depths and functional architecture. Represents a distributed support-separation project, not a literal technical map. No lettering, symbols as text, captions, watermark.
